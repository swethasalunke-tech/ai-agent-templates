Billing FAQ (Example)

Do you offer annual billing?
Yes. Annual billing is available on the Starter, Pro, and Enterprise plans and includes a 15% discount versus paying monthly. You can switch from monthly to annual billing at any time from Account Settings > Billing; the switch takes effect at your next renewal date.

What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major credit cards and, for Enterprise plans, ACH bank transfer and invoicing with net-30 terms.

Can I get an invoice instead of a receipt?
Yes, itemized invoices are available for all plans under Account Settings > Billing > Invoices.

What happens if a payment fails?
We retry a failed payment three times over seven days. If all retries fail, the account is downgraded to the Free plan until billing is resolved.
