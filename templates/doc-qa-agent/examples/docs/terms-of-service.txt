Terms of Service (Example)

1. Accounts
You must provide accurate information when creating an account and are responsible for activity under your account.

2. Billing and Refunds
Annual plans may be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans may be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Refund requests must be sent to support@example.com and are processed within 5 business days. After the applicable window, subscriptions are non-refundable but can be cancelled to stop future billing.

3. Trial Period
New accounts on the Pro and Enterprise plans include a 14-day free trial. No payment method is required to start a trial. If a payment method is added and the trial is not cancelled, billing begins automatically on day 15.

4. Cancellation
You may cancel at any time from Account Settings > Billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; there is no partial-period refund outside the refund windows above.

5. Data Retention
Account data is retained for 90 days after cancellation, after which it is permanently deleted.
